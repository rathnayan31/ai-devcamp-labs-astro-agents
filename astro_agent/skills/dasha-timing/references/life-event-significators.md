# Life-event significators

| Event | Houses | Lords to check | Karaka(s) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Marriage | 7 (also 2, 11) | 7th lord | Venus; Jupiter for a husband |
| Children | 5 (also 9) | 5th lord | Jupiter |
| Career / job change | 10 (also 6 for service) | 10th lord | Saturn, Sun, Mercury |
| Business | 7, 10, 11 | 7th & 10th lords | Mercury |
| Wealth / income | 2, 11 | 2nd & 11th lords | Jupiter, Venus |
| Property / vehicle | 4 | 4th lord | Mars (land), Venus (vehicle) |
| Foreign travel / settlement | 12, 9 (also 3 short trips) | 12th & 9th lords | Rahu |
| Education | 4, 5, 9 | 5th lord | Mercury, Jupiter |
| Health crises | 6, 8, 12 | 6th & 8th lords | Saturn; Sun for vitality |

Typical windows for one-time events, used to decide tense:
- Marriage: usually by the late 20s or early 30s.
- First child: after marriage, usually within its following dasha sub-periods.

A window is strongest when the mahadasha lord and the antardasha lord are both
significators, and when the significator is a functional benefic for the
ascendant (see the chart-interpretation skill).
